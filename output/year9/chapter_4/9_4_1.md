### Key Vocabulary
- **Algebraic fraction**: A fraction in which the numerator, denominator, or both contain algebraic pronumerals, such as $\frac{2x - 3}{5}$.
- **Lowest Common Denominator (LCD)**: The lowest common multiple (LCM) of all denominators in an equation, used to clear fractions in a single multiplication step.
- **Literal equation (Formula)**: An equation involving two or more variables (pronumerals), such as $v = u + at$ or $A = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$.
- **Subject of a formula**: The single variable isolated on one side of a formula with a coefficient of $1$, expressed in terms of the remaining variables.

## Theory & Key Ideas

### Clearing Fractional Denominators Using the LCD

When solving a linear equation containing fractions, multiply every term on both sides of the equation by the lowest common denominator (LCD) of all fractions. Because a fraction bar acts as an invisible bracket around its numerator, always enclose binomial numerators in brackets before expanding.

$$\frac{ax + b}{m} - \frac{cx + d}{n} = k \implies \text{Multiply all terms by } \text{lcm}(m, n)$$

> **💡 Bracket Binomial Numerators Immediately**
> A subtraction sign in front of a fraction applies to the entire numerator: $-\frac{2x - 5}{3}$ becomes $-(2x - 5) = -2x + 5$ after clearing the denominator.

### Cross-Multiplication for Single-Fraction Proportions

When an equation has the form of one single fraction equal to another single fraction (where denominators are non-zero), multiplying both sides by the product of the denominators yields the cross-multiplication rule.

$$\frac{A}{B} = \frac{C}{D} \implies AD = BC \quad (B \neq 0,\; D \neq 0)$$

> **💡 Only Cross-Multiply Single Fractions**
> Cross-multiplication only applies when there is a single fraction on the left-hand side and a single fraction on the right-hand side.

### Transposing Literal Equations and Formulas

To make a specified variable the subject of a literal equation or scientific formula, apply inverse operations in reverse order of operations (SAMDOB), treating all other pronumerals as constants. If the target variable appears in more than one term, collect those terms on one side and factorise.

$$v^2 = u^2 + 2as \implies 2as = v^2 - u^2 \implies a = \frac{v^2 - u^2}{2s}$$

> **💡 Treat Other Pronumerals Like Numbers**
> Every inverse step used for numerical equations—clearing fractions, expanding brackets, subtracting terms, and dividing by the coefficient—works identically for literal equations.

## Worked Examples

### WE9_4_1_1: Solving a Linear Equation with Binomial Fraction Numerators

**Problem**: Solve the linear equation $\frac{2x + 1}{3} - \frac{x - 4}{2} = 5$.

| Step | Thinking | Working |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | The denominators are $3$ and $2$, so the LCD is $\text{lcm}(3, 2) = 6$. Multiply every term on both sides by $6$, placing brackets around each binomial numerator. | $$6 \times \frac{2x + 1}{3} - 6 \times \frac{x - 4}{2} = 6 \times 5$$
$$2(2x + 1) - 3(x - 4) = 30$$ |
| 2 | Expand the brackets carefully (noting $-3 \times (-4) = +12$), collect like terms, and solve for $x$. | $$4x + 2 - 3x + 12 = 30$$
$$x + 14 = 30 \implies x = 16$$ |

**Answer**: $x = 16$

> **✏️ Now You Try**
> **Problem**: Solve $\frac{3x - 2}{4} - \frac{x - 5}{3} = 4$.
> *Hint*: Multiply every term by the LCD $12$: $3(3x - 2) - 4(x - 5) = 48$.
> **Working**: $9x - 6 - 4x + 20 = 48 \implies 5x + 14 = 48 \implies 5x = 34 \implies x = \frac{34}{5} = 6.8$.
> **Answer**: $x = \frac{34}{5}$ (or $6.8$)

### WE9_4_1_2: Solving Rational Equations Using Cross-Multiplication

**Problem**: Solve $\frac{4x - 3}{5} = \frac{2x + 3}{3}$.

| Step | Thinking | Working |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Since a single fraction equals a single fraction, cross-multiply by multiplying both sides by $15$. | $$3(4x - 3) = 5(2x + 3)$$ |
| 2 | Expand both sides, subtract $10x$ from both sides, and solve for $x$. | $$12x - 9 = 10x + 15$$
$$2x = 24 \implies x = 12$$ |

**Answer**: $x = 12$

> **✏️ Now You Try**
> **Problem**: Solve $\frac{5x - 1}{4} = \frac{3x + 5}{3}$.
> *Hint*: Cross-multiply to obtain $3(5x - 1) = 4(3x + 5)$.
> **Working**: $15x - 3 = 12x + 20 \implies 3x = 23 \implies x = \frac{23}{3}$.
> **Answer**: $x = \frac{23}{3}$

### WE9_4_1_3: Transposing the Trapezium Area Formula

**Problem**: The area $A$ of a trapezium with parallel sides $a$ and $b$ and perpendicular height $h$ is given by $A = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$. (a) Transpose the formula to make $b$ the subject. (b) Hence find $b$ when $A = 54\text{ cm}^2$, $h = 6\text{ cm}$, and $a = 7\text{ cm}$.

| Step | Thinking | Working |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Multiply both sides by $2$ to clear the fraction, then divide both sides by $h$, and finally subtract $a$. | $$2A = (a + b)h \implies \frac{2A}{h} = a + b \implies b = \frac{2A}{h} - a$$ |
| 2 | Substitute $A = 54$, $h = 6$, and $a = 7$ into the transposed formula. | $$b = \frac{2(54)}{6} - 7 = \frac{108}{6} - 7 = 18 - 7 = 11\text{ cm}$$ |

**Answer**: (a) $b = \frac{2A}{h} - a$, (b) $b = 11\text{ cm}$

> **✏️ Now You Try**
> **Problem**: The temperature conversion formula from Celsius ($C$) to Fahrenheit ($F$) is $F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32$. (a) Make $C$ the subject. (b) Find $C$ when $F = 95$.
> *Hint*: Subtract $32$ first, then multiply the result by $\frac{5}{9}$.
> **Working**: (a) $C = \frac{5(F - 32)}{9}$; (b) $C = \frac{5(95 - 32)}{9} = \frac{5 \times 63}{9} = 35^\circ\text{C}$.
> **Answer**: (a) $C = \frac{5(F - 32)}{9}$, (b) $C = 35$

## ⚠️ Common Misconceptions Alert

### Failing to Distribute a Negative Sign Across a Binomial Numerator
- **The Misconception**: When subtracting an algebraic fraction such as $-\frac{x - 4}{2}$, students often multiply to get $-3x - 12$ instead of $-3(x - 4) = -3x + 12$.
- **The Correct Concept**: The vinculum (fraction bar) groups the entire numerator as if it were inside brackets. Always write the brackets explicitly before expanding.
- **❌ Incorrect Working**: $$6 \times \frac{2x + 1}{3} - 6 \times \frac{x - 4}{2} = 2(2x + 1) - 3x - 4 \quad \text{(Incorrect)}$$
- **✅ Correct Working**: $$6 \times \frac{2x + 1}{3} - 6 \times \frac{x - 4}{2} = 2(2x + 1) - 3(x - 4) = 4x + 2 - 3x + 12$$
- **Counter-Example**: If $x = 16$, then $\frac{2(16)+1}{3} - \frac{16-4}{2} = \frac{33}{3} - \frac{12}{2} = 11 - 6 = 5$, which matches the RHS only when $-3(-4) = +12$ is used.

### Forgetting to Multiply Integer Terms When Clearing Fractions
- **The Misconception**: Multiplying only the fractional terms by the LCD and leaving non-fraction constant terms unchanged.
- **The Correct Concept**: To preserve equality, every term on both sides of the equation—including integers—must be multiplied by the LCD.
- **❌ Incorrect Working**: $$\frac{x}{3} + 2 = 5 \implies x + 2 = 15 \quad \text{(Incorrect: } 2 \text{ was not multiplied by } 3\text{)}$$
- **✅ Correct Working**: $$3 \times \frac{x}{3} + 3 \times 2 = 3 \times 5 \implies x + 6 = 15 \implies x = 9$$
- **Counter-Example**: Substituting $x = 9$ into $\frac{x}{3} + 2$ gives $\frac{9}{3} + 2 = 3 + 2 = 5$.

Solve $\frac{3x - 5}{4} = 7$.

#### Question `9_4_1_Q2` [Medium] *(Fluency)*
Solve $\frac{x + 2}{3} + \frac{x + 1}{4} = 5$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiply every term by $\text{lcm}(3, 4) = 12$: $4(x + 2) + 3(x + 1) = 60$.
2. Expand and simplify: $4x + 8 + 3x + 3 = 60 \implies 7x + 11 = 60 \implies 7x = 49 \implies x = 7$.

**Final Answer**: $x = 7$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q3` [Medium] *(Fluency)*
Solve $\frac{2x - 1}{3} - \frac{x - 5}{6} = 4$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiply every term by the LCD $6$: $2(2x - 1) - (x - 5) = 24$.
2. Expand and solve: $4x - 2 - x + 5 = 24 \implies 3x + 3 = 24 \implies 3x = 21 \implies x = 7$.

**Final Answer**: $x = 7$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q4` [Medium] *(Understanding)*
Transpose the linear motion formula $v = u + at$ to make $t$ the subject, and find $t$ when $v = 32$, $u = 8$, and $a = 6$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Subtract $u$ from both sides: $v - u = at$, then divide by $a$: $t = \frac{v - u}{a}$.
2. Substitute $v = 32, u = 8, a = 6$: $t = \frac{32 - 8}{6} = \frac{24}{6} = 4$.

**Final Answer**: $t = \frac{v - u}{a}$, $t = 4$
</details>

### Problem-Solving & Modelling

#### Question `9_4_1_Q5` [Medium] *(Kinematics Modelling)*
A cyclist rides from Geelong to Lorne. She travels the first part of a training circuit at $20\text{ km/h}$ for $x\text{ km}$ and returns along an $x + 15\text{ km}$ coastal route at $30\text{ km/h}$. The total riding time is $3\text{ hours}$, modelled by $\frac{x}{20} + \frac{x + 15}{30} = 3$. Solve for $x$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiply every term by $\text{lcm}(20, 30) = 60$: $3x + 2(x + 15) = 180$.
2. Expand and solve: $3x + 2x + 30 = 180 \implies 5x = 150 \implies x = 30\text{ km}$.

**Final Answer**: $x = 30\text{ km}$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q6` [Challenging] *(Australian Trade Pricing)*
An electrician in Ballarat charges a call-out fee of $\$F$ plus $\$r$ per hour for $h$ hours, plus $10\%$ GST on the total bill, so the final invoice $C$ is $C = \frac{11}{10}(F + rh)$. (a) Rearrange the formula to make $h$ the subject. (b) Find $h$ if $C = \$429$, $F = \$90$, and $r = \$75$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiply by $10$ and divide by $11$: $\frac{10C}{11} = F + rh \implies rh = \frac{10C}{11} - F = \frac{10C - 11F}{11} \implies h = \frac{10C - 11F}{11r}$.
2. Substitute $C = 429, F = 90, r = 75$: $h = \frac{10(429) - 11(90)}{11(75)} = \frac{4290 - 990}{825} = \frac{3300}{825} = 4\text{ hours}$.

**Final Answer**: (a) $h = \frac{10C - 11F}{11r}$, (b) $h = 4\text{ hours}$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q7` [Challenging] *(Rate Modelling)*
Two inlet pipes fill a rainwater tank in Bendigo. Pipe A alone fills the tank in $6\text{ hours}$ and Pipe B alone fills it in $4\text{ hours}$. Together, the time $t\text{ hours}$ required to fill the tank satisfies $\frac{t}{6} + \frac{t}{4} = 1$. Find $t$ in hours and minutes.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiply every term by $\text{lcm}(6, 4) = 12$: $2t + 3t = 12$.
2. Solve for $t$: $5t = 12 \implies t = \frac{12}{5} = 2.4\text{ hours}$, which is $2\text{ hours } 24\text{ minutes}$.

**Final Answer**: $t = \frac{12}{5}\text{ hours}$ ($2\text{ hours } 24\text{ minutes}$)
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q8` [Challenging] *(Measurement Formulas)*
The total surface area $S$ of a closed cylinder of radius $r$ and height $h$ is $S = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh$. (a) Transpose the formula to make $h$ the subject. (b) Find the exact value of $h$ when $S = 96\pi\text{ cm}^2$ and $r = 4\text{ cm}$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Subtract $2\pi r^2$ from both sides: $S - 2\pi r^2 = 2\pi rh$, then divide by $2\pi r$: $h = \frac{S - 2\pi r^2}{2\pi r}$.
2. Substitute $S = 96\pi$ and $r = 4$: $h = \frac{96\pi - 2\pi(16)}{2\pi(4)} = \frac{64\pi}{8\pi} = 8\text{ cm}$.

**Final Answer**: (a) $h = \frac{S - 2\pi r^2}{2\pi r}$, (b) $h = 8\text{ cm}$
</details>

### Reasoning & Communication

#### Question `9_4_1_Q9` [Medium] *(Error Analysis)*
Liam attempts to solve $\frac{5x - 2}{3} - \frac{x - 6}{3} = 4$ and writes $5x - 2 - x - 6 = 12 \implies 4x - 8 = 12 \implies x = 5$. Identify Liam's error and find the correct solution.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Multiplying by $3$ gives $(5x - 2) - (x - 6) = 12$. Distributing the negative sign across $(x - 6)$ yields $-x + 6$, not $-x - 6$.
2. Solving correctly: $5x - 2 - x + 6 = 12 \implies 4x + 4 = 12 \implies 4x = 8 \implies x = 2$.

**Final Answer**: Liam wrote $-6$ instead of $+6$ when subtracting $-(x - 6)$; the correct answer is $x = 2$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q10` [Challenging] *(Domain Restrictions)*
Explain why the equation $\frac{6}{x - 3} = \frac{2}{x - 3} + 4$ cannot have $x = 3$ as a valid solution, and solve the equation to find the valid root.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. If $x = 3$, the denominator $x - 3$ equals $0$, and division by zero is undefined.
2. For $x \neq 3$, multiply all terms by $(x - 3)$: $6 = 2 + 4(x - 3) \implies 4 = 4(x - 3) \implies x - 3 = 1 \implies x = 4$.

**Final Answer**: $x = 3$ causes division by zero; the valid solution is $x = 4$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q11` [Challenging] *(Algebraic Reasoning)*
To make $x$ the subject of $ax + b = cx + d$ (where $a \neq c$), explain why all terms containing $x$ must be collected on one side and factorised.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Subtract $cx$ and $b$ to collect $x$-terms on the LHS: $ax - cx = d - b$.
2. Because $a$ and $c$ are non-like pronumerals, $ax - cx$ must be factorised as $x(a - c) = d - b$, allowing division by $(a - c)$ to isolate $x = \frac{d - b}{a - c}$.

**Final Answer**: Factorising $x(a - c) = d - b$ combines the two $x$ terms into a single occurrence of $x$, giving $x = \frac{d - b}{a - c}$
</details>

#### Question `9_4_1_Q12` [Extension] *(Literal Equations & Proof)*
The harmonic mean $H$ of two positive numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfies $\frac{2}{H} = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$. (a) Show that $H = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$. (b) Rearrange this formula to make $a$ the subject in terms of $H$ and $b$, and evaluate $a$ when $H = 12$ and $b = 15$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Full Step-by-Step Solution</strong></summary>

1. Add the fractions on the RHS over the common denominator $ab$: $\frac{2}{H} = \frac{a + b}{ab}$. Taking the reciprocal of both sides and multiplying by $2$ gives $H = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$.
2. To isolate $a$: $H(a + b) = 2ab \implies Ha + Hb = 2ab \implies Hb = 2ab - Ha = a(2b - H) \implies a = \frac{Hb}{2b - H}$. When $H = 12, b = 15$: $a = \frac{12(15)}{2(15) - 12} = \frac{180}{18} = 10$.

**Final Answer**: (a) $\frac{2}{H} = \frac{b + a}{ab} \implies H = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$, (b) $a = \frac{Hb}{2b - H}$, $a = 10$
</details>
