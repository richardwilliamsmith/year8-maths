Q1. A fair 666-sided die (1,2,3,4,5,61, 2, 3, 4, 5, 61,2,3,4,5,6) is rolled once. Find the probability of rolling:

(c) A number greater than 444 (555 or 666)
(d) A number that is NOT 555

Q3. A Venn diagram of 202020 students shows 888 play Soccer ONLY, 555 play BOTH Soccer and Tennis, and 444 play Tennis ONLY (333 play neither). Find:
(a) Total number of students who play Soccer (8+58 + 58+5)
(b) Total number of students who play Tennis (5+45 + 45+4)
(c) Number of students who play Soccer OR Tennis (8+5+48 + 5 + 48+5+4)
(d) Probability a chosen student plays BOTH sports
