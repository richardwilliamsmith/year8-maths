Q1. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations using the substitution method:
(a) y=3x−2y = 3x - 2y=3x−2 and 2x+y=132x + y = 132x+y=13
(b) y=2x+5y = 2x + 5y=2x+5 and y=5x−4y = 5x - 4y=5x−4
(c) x=4−2yx = 4 - 2yx=4−2y and 3x−2y=43x - 2y = 43x−2y=4
(d) y=7−3xy = 7 - 3xy=7−3x and 4x−3y=54x - 3y = 54x−3y=5
Q2. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations by adding or subtracting to eliminate a variable:
(a) 3x+2y=193x + 2y = 193x+2y=19 and 5x−2y=215x - 2y = 215x−2y=21
(b) 4x+3y=184x + 3y = 184x+3y=18 and 4x−y=104x - y = 104x−y=10
(c) 2x+5y=92x + 5y = 92x+5y=9 and 2x+y=−32x + y = -32x+y=−3
(d) 7x−3y=207x - 3y = 207x−3y=20 and 2x+3y=72x + 3y = 72x+3y=7
Q3. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations using multipliers and the elimination method:
(a) 2x+3y=132x + 3y = 132x+3y=13 and 3x+2y=123x + 2y = 123x+2y=12
(b) 3x−4y=103x - 4y = 103x−4y=10 and 5x+2y=85x + 2y = 85x+2y=8
(c) 4x+3y=114x + 3y = 114x+3y=11 and 3x−2y=13x - 2y = 13x−2y=1
(d) 5x−3y=195x - 3y = 195x−3y=19 and 2x−5y=12x - 5y = 12x−5y=1
