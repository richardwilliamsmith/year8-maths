Q1. Check whether the given point (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) satisfies BOTH equations:
(a) (2,5)(2, 5)(2,5) in x+y=7x + y = 7x+y=7 and y=2x+1y = 2x + 1y=2x+1
(b) (3,1)(3, 1)(3,1) in x+y=4x + y = 4x+y=4 and x−y=2x - y = 2x−y=2
(c) (4,2)(4, 2)(4,2) in x+y=6x + y = 6x+y=6 and 2x+y=82x + y = 82x+y=8
(d) (1,4)(1, 4)(1,4) in y=4xy = 4xy=4x and x+y=5x + y = 5x+y=5
Q2. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations by substituting the first equation into the second:
(a) y=2xy = 2xy=2x and x+y=9x + y = 9x+y=9 (x+2x=9x + 2x = 9x+2x=9)
(b) y=3xy = 3xy=3x and 2x+y=102x + y = 102x+y=10 (2x+3x=102x + 3x = 102x+3x=10)
(c) y=x+2y = x + 2y=x+2 and x+y=8x + y = 8x+y=8 (x+(x+2)=8x + (x + 2) = 8x+(x+2)=8)
(d) y=x−1y = x - 1y=x−1 and 2x+y=112x + y = 112x+y=11 (2x+(x−1)=112x + (x - 1) = 112x+(x−1)=11)
Q3. Add the two equations together to eliminate yyy, then find xxx and yyy:
(a) x+y=7x + y = 7x+y=7 and x−y=3x - y = 3x−y=3 (2x=102x = 102x=10)
(b) x+y=10x + y = 10x+y=10 and x−y=2x - y = 2x−y=2 (2x=122x = 122x=12)
(c) 2x+y=92x + y = 92x+y=9 and x−y=3x - y = 3x−y=3 (3x=123x = 123x=12)
(d) 3x+y=113x + y = 113x+y=11 and 2x−y=42x - y = 42x−y=4 (5x=155x = 155x=15)
Q4. Subtract the second equation from the first to eliminate xxx, then find xxx and yyy:
(a) x+3y=11x + 3y = 11x+3y=11 and x+y=5x + y = 5x+y=5 (2y=62y = 62y=6)
(b) 2x+3y=132x + 3y = 132x+3y=13 and 2x+y=72x + y = 72x+y=7 (2y=62y = 62y=6)
(c) 3x+4y=183x + 4y = 183x+4y=18 and 3x+y=93x + y = 93x+y=9 (3y=93y = 93y=9)
(d) 4x+5y=224x + 5y = 224x+5y=22 and 4x+2y=164x + 2y = 164x+2y=16 (3y=63y = 63y=6)

Q7.
