Q1. A fair coin is tossed twice. The 444 equally likely outcomes are HH,HT,TH,TTHH, HT, TH, TTHH,HT,TH,TT. Find the probability of getting:
(a) Two Heads (HHHHHH)
(b) Two Tails (TTTTTT)
(c) Exactly one Head and one Tail (HTHTHT or THTHTH)
(d) At least one Head (HH,HTHH, HTHH,HT, or THTHTH)
Q2. A fair coin (H,TH, TH,T) is tossed and a fair 666-sided die (1,2,3,4,5,61, 2, 3, 4, 5, 61,2,3,4,5,6) is rolled. Find:
(a) The total number of outcomes in the grid (2×62 \times 62×6)
(b) The probability of getting (H,6)(H, 6)(H,6)
(c) The probability of getting Heads and an even number ((H,2),(H,4),(H,6)(H,2), (H,4), (H,6)(H,2),(H,4),(H,6))
(d) The probability of getting Tails and a number less than 333 ((T,1),(T,2)(T,1), (T,2)(T,1),(T,2))

(b) Pr⁡(R)\Pr(R)Pr(R) on the second draw (since the counter was replaced)
